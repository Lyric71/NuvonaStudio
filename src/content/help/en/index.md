Nuvora is a workspace for LinkedIn and nothing else. It writes your posts with AI or lets you write them yourself, publishes them on your profile or on the company pages you administer, lays them out on a calendar, reads your LinkedIn ad account live, has posts approved by a teammate or a client, answers your questions about all of it, and sets agents to watch it for you, for you alone or for your whole team, paid from one prepaid balance. These guides follow the menu, from your first post to your team, your clients and your invoices.

## Start here

1. [Getting started](/help/getting-started): what Nuvora is, creating your account (country, Just me, Create a team or Join a team, how you heard about us), signing in, the menu, and your first post.
2. [Discovery and tips](/help/discovery-and-tips): the Discovery page your first sign-in opens on, its modules and first steps, the quick tour of the screen, and the tips that come after you sign in.

## Publish on LinkedIn

3. [LinkedIn posts](/help/linkedin-posts): briefing and drafting a post, its copy and versions, a picture or a carousel, Made for a client, then publishing it on your profile or a company page, now, on schedule or by hand.
4. [Calendar](/help/calendar): every post planned, scheduled, published or refused, by month, by week or as a list, and the publishing findings under it.
5. [LinkedIn Ads](/help/linkedin-ads): the ad account read live on the Dashboard, campaigns switched on and off and their budgets changed, and the Ad Library.
6. [Validation](/help/validation): sending a post or a file for approval to a teammate or a client, deciding, new versions and comments.

## Ask and agents

7. [Ask](/help/ask): questions answered from your posts, your calendar, your ad account, your files and the validation queue, with the data included, web search, models, skills and kept conversations.
8. [Agents](/help/agents): the LinkedIn publishing watcher and the LinkedIn Ads watcher, your own agents, schedules, runs, findings and what a run costs.

## Library and skills

9. [Assets Library](/help/assets-library): every file of the team in folders, finding, tagging and moving them, adding them to a campaign, and the Image editor that frames a picture for LinkedIn, crops, adjusts, writes on it and places a logo.
10. [Campaigns](/help/campaigns): the visuals, documents and posts of one LinkedIn push gathered under a name and a brief, then read as a whole by Ask, your agents and your posts.
11. [Skills](/help/skills): the instructions that shape your drafts and your answers in Ask, the Catalog, your own skills and team skills.
12. [Choosing a model](/help/choosing-a-model): the Quick, Balanced and Best choices in posts, the rewrite menu and Ask, what a run costs, All models, and the Model benchmarks page that compares the text models on public benchmarks.

## Your team and your clients

13. [Your team](/help/your-team): working alone or in a team, the Admin, Creator, Viewer and Client roles, invitations, requests to join, daily limits, pausing a login, clients and their logins, invoice details and the sign-in code.
14. [Client space](/help/client-space): for the people of your clients: what they see, downloading, and approving the team's work.

## Your account and your money

15. [My Connections](/help/my-connections): connecting your LinkedIn profile, your company pages and your ad accounts, how long a connection lasts, renewing it, and the connection check after you sign in.
16. [Balance and payments](/help/balance-and-payments): a prepaid balance in US dollars, the team balance then your own, topping up by card, Alipay or WeChat Pay, promotional codes, automatic top-up, invoices and the usage log.
17. [Account and sign-in](/help/account-and-sign-in): your name, picture, email and password, light or dark, the interface in English, French or Chinese, date and time, your home page, My models, voice input, the sign-in code by email, password reset and what happens to a login left unused.
18. [Partners](/help/partners): the partner area, for commercial partners only.

## When something goes wrong

19. [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting): the messages Nuvora shows, what they mean and what to do.

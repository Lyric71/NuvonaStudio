Custom engagement

Enterprise

For global companies that need LinkedIn running across markets, teams, and languages.

Our standard tiers fit most B2B companies. Some organizations need more. Multiple regions. Dozens of executive profiles. Content in four languages. Ad campaigns running in parallel across APAC, EMEA, and the Americas.

That is what Enterprise is for.

We build a scope around your structure. Not a one-size package with your logo on it. Every Enterprise engagement starts with a discovery call to map your goals, markets, stakeholders, and internal workflows.

What Enterprise clients typically need

  • Multi-market LinkedIn strategy spanning several regions and languages
  • Coordinated content calendars across company pages and executive profiles
  • Large-scale employee advocacy programs for global teams
  • Dedicated senior strategist as your single point of contact
  • LinkedIn Ads management across multiple ad accounts and currencies
  • Multilingual content production, written by people who understand the market, not just the language
  • Custom reporting dashboards aligned to your internal KPIs
  • Quarterly business reviews with senior leadership

How pricing works

Enterprise is scoped and quoted per engagement. No fixed price, because no two programmes look the same. Pricing is built around the markets, profiles, content volume, ad accounts, and languages in scope.

Most Enterprise engagements start between $10,000 and $25,000 a month. Some run larger. You'll get a clear, line-item proposal before anything kicks off. No surprises.

Who this is built for

  • Global companies with teams across multiple countries
  • Organisations expanding into new markets, across any industry
  • Companies where LinkedIn is a strategic channel, not a side project

If your company has outgrown a single retainer tier, this is the conversation to have next.
